## V06：A字牌 / 路边立牌（最急）

**用途**：放在人行道上，梧桐树挡了门头，这是唯一能伸出去的东西
**尺寸**：1024×1536（竖版 2:3）
**类型**：图形设计 + 摄影融合

```
A-frame sidewalk sign design for a small noodle shop. The sign sits on a real Shanghai street with plane trees in soft background blur.

The sign itself: rustic wooden chalkboard style A-frame, standing on the sidewalk. On the board surface, a clean modern design with Chinese text "铜锅现煮·往里走" in bold warm typography, and smaller text "三种酸·自己腌的". A simple line-drawing illustration of a steaming copper pot beside the text.

Setting: daytime urban street, University Road atmosphere, plane tree shadows on pavement, warm natural light. The sign is the hero element, sharp focus.

Style: editorial graphic design, premium but approachable, craft aesthetic, wood + chalk + warm typography. NOT corporate, NOT fast food chain, NOT neon.

Mood: inviting, cozy, "there's something good hidden here", warm and authentic.

Technical: 4K, photorealistic rendering of the sign in real environment, 2:3 vertical.
```

## V07：三种酸小卡片（每碗附一张）

**用途**：食客上米线时附送，拍照传播的核心物料
**尺寸**：1024×1024（1:1，正反面可拼一张）
**类型**：平面设计稿

```
Flat lay graphic design mockup of a small kraft paper card, photographed on a rustic wooden table with soft window light.

The card design: cream kraft paper, minimal layout. Three hand-drawn line illustrations — one fermented mustard greens, one pickled chive flowers, one chili peppers. Below each, small Chinese labels: "酸腌菜" "韭菜花" "泡椒". The card has a subtle torn edge, artisan feel. Centered at top, small text: "云味·三种酸". Bottom small text: "云南老坛发酵·自己腌的".

Beside the card on the table: a few scattered fresh herbs, a pair of wooden chopsticks, soft natural shadows.

Style: Japanese craft aesthetic meets Chinese typography, minimal, lots of negative space, risograph print texture suggestion, handmade paper feel.

Mood: warm, authentic, artisanal, "someone made this by hand", collected-worthy.

Technical: 4K, overhead flat lay, 1:1 square, photorealistic product mockup. Chinese text should be clearly legible but minimal.
```

**用途**：店内A3海报，解释"写故事换米线"活动规则
**尺寸**：1024×1536（竖版 2:3）
**类型**：平面设计海报

```
Vertical poster design for a noodle shop campaign, displayed on a warm wood-paneled wall with soft ambient lighting.

The poster itself: elegant editorial design, cream paper background with subtle texture. Layout from top to bottom:

Top section: Large Chinese headline "复旦同济中间的那锅酸" in bold display typography, slightly offset, editorial style.

Middle section: Three horizontally arranged small illustration blocks — a copper pot icon, a wall of paper notes icon, a bowl of noodles icon — each with one-line description in small Chinese text.

Bottom section: campaign rules in clean Chinese typography — "写一个故事，换一碗米线" as the main call to action, with smaller details about dates and how to participate.

Color palette: warm earth tones — terracotta clay, copper gold, cream, deep ink black for text. One accent: a muted pickled-green.

Style: modern Chinese editorial poster, magazine-quality, lots of negative space, Bauhaus-meets-tea-house aesthetic, typography-forward, NOT commercial flyer, NOT supermarket poster.

Mood: literary, warm, sophisticated but inviting, "this is not a normal noodle shop".

Technical: 4K, 2:3 vertical, photorealistic poster-on-wall mockup. Chinese text should be clean and readable.

## V11：学生证专属卡

**用途**：复旦同济学生凭证到店专属优惠卡设计
**尺寸**：1024×1024（1:1，正反面拼一张）
**类型**：平面设计稿

```
Flat lay graphic design of a student exclusive card for a noodle shop near Fudan and Tongji universities.

The card: credit-card proportion, kraft paper texture. Front face: centered illustration of a steaming copper pot in line-drawing style. Above it, "云味" in Chinese calligraphy. Below the pot, "学生专属" in clean typography. A small line: "凭学生证到店". Subtle watermark-like background pattern of plane tree leaves.

The card is photographed resting on a wooden table, with a university student ID card partially visible in the corner for context, soft natural window light, warm shadows.

Style: Japanese membership card aesthetic, minimal, craft feel, letterpress texture suggestion, numbered-edition feeling. NOT commercial loyalty card, NOT plastic gift card.

Mood: exclusive, "I want to keep this card", collectible, warm and personal.

Technical: 4K, 1:1 square, photorealistic flat lay mockup. Chinese text minimal and legible.
